With a race start entered, every time is shown and entered as clock time, with race time since start in small text underneath. Without it, times are race clock.
Settings belong to this race and are shared with everyone who opens it.
Type the pit-in time in the outlined cell of the next stint (h:mm:ss, clock time), then its pit-out time once the car leaves. Changes save to the shared database; other devices see them after Refresh (live updates come in a later step). Tap any recorded time to correct it, or × to remove a stop. Planned pit-in is the latest legal lap boundary (max stint minus buffer, moved earlier around closed windows). Window is the earliest pit-in that avoids an extra stop, to the latest.
